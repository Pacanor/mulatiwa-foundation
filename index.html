Why Mulatiwa

Change takes more than one intervention.

Mulatiwa Foundation is a non-governmental organisation working toward the holistic transformation of young people, elderly people and disadvantaged communities.

We connect quality learning with leadership, economic independence, wellbeing and local action—because people thrive when the whole ecosystem around them grows stronger.

The Foundation is the parent organisation. Mulatiwa Academy is its flagship girls’ leadership school, now in planning. Our mandate also includes families, elders and underserved communities across Zambia, from a home in Lusaka.
